# Cases of right-to-left layout, in the format of layout-input.txt with two more keys: rtl on p makes the paragraph right to left,
# rtl on r makes the run right to left. Align is the side of the page, so a right-to-left paragraph that starts at the edge it
# starts from says align=right; left, right and first count from the start edge (the right one) and the end edge.
# Every family is the script font of ../shaping/script.ttf (Hebrew, a few Arabic letters, Latin, digits).
case alignment of a right to left paragraph
section
page 200 200 20 20 20 20
p rtl align=right
r | אבג דהו
p rtl align=left
r | אבג דהו
p rtl align=center
r | אבג דהו
p rtl
r | אבג דהו
case mixed text in a left to right paragraph
section
page 200 200 20 20 20 20
p
r | abc אבג def 12 דהו
p align=right
r | abc אבג דהו def
case digits and brackets in right to left text
section

p rtl align=right
