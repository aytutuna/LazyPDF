pdf.js (https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js)
    Copyright 2012 Mozilla Foundation. Apache License 2.0.
    The JBIG2 decoder of Lazy PDF (LazyPDF.Core/Filters/Jbig2.cs, Jbig2Arith.cs,
    Jbig2Generic.cs, Jbig2Halftone.cs, Jbig2Huffman.cs, Jbig2Symbols.cs and
    Jbig2Text.cs, and their Swift twins of the same names in
    Sources/LazyPDFKit/Filters) is ported from
    pdf.js src/core/jbig2.js and src/core/arithmetic_decoder.js, version
    4.10.38, and changed: it is written in C# and Swift, reads Group 4 data
    through the engine's own CCITT decoder, takes the generic region template bits in the
    order of ITU-T T.88, and adds the parts pdf.js lacks (Huffman coded
    symbol refinement, skipped halftone cells, typical prediction of
    refinement rows, the other combination operators, refinement region
    segments) and limits on the sizes and counts a stream may ask for. Each of
    those files keeps the copyright notice and the licence header of the
    original and says it was changed.
