3. Fonts that ship with the app

The converter that turns Word, Excel and PowerPoint files into PDF uses these
fonts when a document names Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman or
Courier New and the PC does not have them. Their letters are as wide as the
fonts they stand for, so lines and pages break where they did in Office. They
are in the Fonts folder next to the app, regular, bold, italic and bold italic,
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose text follows the copyright lines.
The fonts are used as they are.
